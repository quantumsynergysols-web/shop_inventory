Made for real handwriting

It reads against your own stock list, and you have the last word.

Shop handwriting is hard: short forms, inch marks, each supplier’s own names for things. So the app matches what it reads to the items already in your catalogue instead of guessing in the open.

  • Matched to your catalogue

    “8 gang board” becomes Switch Board 8-gang from your stock, with close alternatives to pick from.

  • Learns each supplier’s words

    Correct a match once and that name is remembered for that supplier next time.

  • The photo beside every row

    Compare the reading with the writing itself, line by line, without going back to the paper.

  • The sums are checked

    Quantity × rate against the amount, rows against the bill total, and gaps in row numbers are all flagged.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is the AI always right?

No. Messy handwriting, folded paper or poor light can fool it. That is why every row is shown beside its photo, unsure rows and totals that don’t add up are marked, and nothing reaches your stock until you confirm.

What do I need to start?

A smartphone with a browser and internet. A thermal printer (58 or 80 mm) is optional: receipts can also be printed on A4 or sent on WhatsApp.

What happens when the internet drops?

A bill you scan is kept on the phone and sent automatically when the connection returns. Reading the bill and updating stock happen once it reaches the server.

Is the receipt an FBR tax invoice?

No. The receipt is for your customer and your own records, and it says on it that it is not an FBR-verified tax invoice. The app is not connected to FBR.

Can I bring in the stock I already have?

Yes. Fill the Excel or CSV template with items, quantities and costs. The app checks every row and shows the mistakes before anything is imported.

Who can see my profit and my data?

Inside your shop, only the people you give a role, and profit only to the owner and accountant. Your shop’s data is kept apart from other shops in the database, backed up daily, and you can export all of it.
