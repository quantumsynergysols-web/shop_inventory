| Step | Narration |
|---|---|
| 01 | This is Shop Inventory ERP, built for shops in Pakistan. It opens in Urdu, and one tap switches it to English. The owner logs in with a phone number and password. |
| 02 | The home screen shows the stock value, today's purchases and sales, the day's profit, and anything running low, all at a glance. |
| 03 | A supplier's handwritten bill has arrived. Tap Purchase and take a photo of it. |
| 04 | The AI reads every line: the item, the quantity, the rate and the total. |
| 05 | Here the AI skipped line five, and the total doesn't match. Instead of trusting it blindly, the app warns you straight away. |
| 06 | Every line shows its own crop of the photo, and the supplier was recognised from the name on the bill. |
| 07 | We add the missing line ourselves. The dinner set is a new product, so we create it right here: six sets at seven thousand five hundred rupees. |
| 08 | Now the lines add up to the bill total. Saving posts all the stock in a single transaction. |
| 09 | Every item shows its stock before and after, and the purchase price and the supplier's balance are updated too. |
| 10 | Next, a customer's sale bill. Tap Sale and take its photo. |
| 11 | The bill asks for sixty tee joints, but only forty-five are in stock, so the app blocks the sale. We change the quantity to forty-five. |
| 12 | The customer, Rana Builders, was matched from the photo. He pays thirty thousand rupees in cash, and the rest goes on his credit account. |
| 13 | The sale is saved. After the remaining stock and the low-stock warnings comes the receipt, ready to print or share on WhatsApp. |
| 14 | At the end of the day comes the day-end closing: cash, bank and credit sales, profit and expenses, all in one place. We add a tea expense. |
| 15 | Now we count the cash drawer and enter the amount. It's two hundred rupees short, and the app shows that clearly. |
| 16 | The day is closed. The counted cash becomes tomorrow's opening cash, and a closed day can't be changed. |
| 17 | The ledger shows who owes the shop money, and whom the shop owes. |
| 18 | In the stock list, search by name, alias or code. Low stock shows in red. |
| 19 | And with one tap, the whole app switches back to Urdu. |
| 20 | Shop Inventory ERP. Take a photo of the bill, and the stock updates itself. Thank you. |
